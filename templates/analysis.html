The t-SNE visualizations reveal that the three classes—PNEUMONIA, NORMAL, and COVID19—form somewhat distinct clusters, especially in the train set. COVID19 samples (green) show clearer separation, while PNEUMONIA (blue) and NORMAL (red) overlap more, indicating potential confusion between these two classes. This overlap suggests the model might struggle to distinguish between PNEUMONIA and NORMAL, highlighting the need for better feature learning or class-specific augmentation. The test set shows similar patterns but with slightly more mixing, which could affect generalization.

The dataset shows a clear class imbalance, with PNEUMONIA heavily overrepresented and COVID19 underrepresented. This can lead to model bias and poor performance on minority classes.
